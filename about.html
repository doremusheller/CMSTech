The perspective behind CMS Tech

Technology should make people more capable.

Craig Schneider is a technologist, educator, and problem solver whose career has moved across enterprise, startup, higher education, and mission-driven organizations. He began building infrastructure at Washington University in St. Louis and has spent his career helping people use technology to do meaningful work better.

That experience informs CMS Tech: clear strategy, practical systems, and teaching that gives people the confidence to use new tools well. The point is never to install technology for its own sake. It is to help real people solve real problems with more clarity, speed, and imagination.

A simple belief

Technology is a tool. People create the value.

A chisel and a paintbrush are technologies. In the hands of people, they become ways to create useful and beautiful things. AI belongs in that lineage: powerful, consequential, and only as good as the human judgment, purpose, and care brought to it.

CMS Tech exists to help leaders and teams apply that care—to understand the tools, choose the right work, and create results worth having.

People firstTechnology should serve the people doing the work.
