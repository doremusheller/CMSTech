Biography

Built for the groove

Shag Fantastiq carries deep mahogany fur with violet-silver undertones, gold-adorned braids, and the sort of confidence that makes a room lean in before the first note. His hybrid instrument, The Thang, bends bass, sax, and didgeridoo into a single low-frequency proposition. He never rushes a groove; he makes time agree with him.
