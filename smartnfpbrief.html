Why charitable operations matter

Lawyers are already a major service resource.

American legal professionals contribute meaningful pro bono time, but participation is uneven and time is the most commonly cited barrier. SmartNFP is designed to make charitable activity easier to organize, measure, and sustain—without replacing human judgment or relationships.

The workflow

Turn activity into better stewardship.

01 / Capture

Donor and gift activity

Bring together email, gifts, forms, engagement, and program participation as the workflow begins.

02 / Access

Microsoft Entra ID

Authorized nonprofit users sign in through secure, role-aware access to the records they need.

03 / Unify

SharePoint and Microsoft 365

Connect donors, programs, gifts, campaigns, and activities through consistent record identifiers.

04 / AI insight

SmartNFP AI

Summarize records, match activity, detect gaps, and recommend useful stewardship or operating actions.

05 / Prioritize

Stewardship Radar

Surface overdue contact, lapsed donors, missing thank-yous, major-gift signals, and follow-up priorities.

06 / Activate

Campaign workspace

Build campaigns, connect them to initiatives, log activity, and coordinate donor follow-up.

07 / Report

Mission Control

Review funding, outcomes, donor participation, campaign progress, and program impact in one view.

08 / Control

Human stewardship and audit

Keep recommendations explainable, actions authorized, and relationship history visible for review.

Built for the real work
