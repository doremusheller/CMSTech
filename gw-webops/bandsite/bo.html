Biography

Built for the groove

Bodacious is the last scion of a fallen interstellar groove dynasty. His radiant copper-gold fur, broad presence, and mischievous grin carry both royal memory and deliberate self-mockery. The crown is cheap on purpose: true rule comes from rhythm, not thrones. With The Solarcaster in hand, he asks empires to dance rather than kneel.
